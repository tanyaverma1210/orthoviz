About OrthoViz

    

    
OrthoViz turns a CT scan into a 3D model to help explain spine anatomy.
      It is a visualization tool, not a diagnostic device. Everything runs on this computer.

    
Anatomical segmentation is performed by TotalSegmentator
      (Apache License 2.0): Wasserthal J. et al., “TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of
      104 Anatomic Structures in CT Images,” Radiology: Artificial Intelligence, 2023,
      doi:10.1148/ryai.230024. TotalSegmentator is built on nnU-Net (Apache License 2.0).
